  My Favorite Movie: As above, So Below
  Movie Information
  Title: As above, So Below
  Release  Year: 2014
  Genre: Horror, Mystery, Thriller
  Director: John Erick Dowdle
  About the Movie:
As Above, So Below is a horror movie about a group of explorers who enter the underground catacombs of Paris in search of a hidden treasure. As they go deeper, they experience terrifying events and discover that the tunnels contain dark secrets connected to their past.
Why I Like This Movie

I like this movie because it combines horror, mystery, and adventure. The underground setting makes the story more frightening and interesting. I also enjoy how the movie keeps viewers curious about what will happen next.

My Favorite Part

My favorite part is when the group discovers the deeper secrets of the catacombs and realizes that escaping is not as easy as they thought.

Rating:
9/10 – A great horror movie for people who enjoy mystery, suspense, and supernatural stories.
